Day by day

WED · NOV 25

Santa Clara → Death Valley

Approx. 470–500 mi · plan 9–11 hr with breaks

Take I‑5 S → CA‑58 E → Barstow → I‑15 N → Baker → CA‑127 N → Shoshone → CA‑190 W to Furnace Creek. Start early and keep the day focused on reaching camp.

Stops: Santa Nella / Buttonwillow for breaks, Barstow for fuel and food, then Baker or Shoshone as needed. Top off before entering the park.

Overnight · Death Valley
THU · NOV 26

Explore Death Valley

A full park day. Focus on Zabriskie Point for sunrise/sunset badlands, Badwater Basin for the salt flats, and Mesquite Flat Sand Dunes for a walk among the dunes.

Use paved park roads and confirm RV parking at each stop. Skip Artists Drive (25-ft vehicle restriction), Titus Canyon Road and Geology Tour Road.

Overnight · Death Valley
FRI · NOV 27

Death Valley → Joshua Tree

Approx. 260 mi · 5.5–6.5 hr with stops

Leave via CA‑190 E → Death Valley Junction → CA‑127 S → Shoshone → Baker → I‑15 S → Barstow → CA‑247 S → Joshua Tree. Keep to the paved southern corridor.

Stops: Shoshone for a break, Baker for fuel/food, and Barstow for supplies. Avoid routing via CA‑178.

Overnight · Joshua Tree
SAT · NOV 28

Explore Joshua Tree

Prioritize Keys View for the broad Coachella Valley panorama, Hidden Valley for its boulder landscape, and Skull Rock / Jumbo Rocks for classic Joshua Tree scenery.

Check parking and access for a 30-ft RV at each stop before committing. Near-full-moon conditions can make faint stars harder to see.

Overnight · Joshua Tree
SUN · NOV 29

Joshua Tree → Malibu / PCH → Ventura → Santa Barbara → Santa Clara

Long return day · allow roughly 11–13 hr including scenic stops and traffic

Leave Joshua Tree early via CA‑62 W / I‑10 W toward Malibu. This passes through the LA metro area, but there is no LA sightseeing or city parking stop planned.

Malibu / PCH: Visit Malibu Pier and Surfrider Beach, then follow Pacific Coast Highway (CA‑1) north past Point Mugu to Ventura Pier. This keeps the coastal section on the route rather than sending you inland toward US‑101 too early.

Santa Barbara: Continue to Stearns Wharf for a waterfront walk. From Santa Barbara, take US‑101 N toward Santa Clara; do not attempt to follow Highway 1 through Big Sur on this return day.

RV parking: The stops are route waypoints, not confirmation that their lots accept a 30-ft RV. Verify oversized-vehicle parking with each location before entering; if a lot cannot fit the RV, skip that stop or use a confirmed alternative. Do not park on a narrow PCH shoulder.

Timing: Aim to leave Joshua Tree around 6:00 AM. Keep Malibu and Ventura stops short and treat Santa Barbara as optional if traffic puts you behind schedule. Check Caltrans conditions and closures on the morning of travel.

Before you roll

Fuel & supplies
  • Start with a full tank.
  • Refuel in Barstow / Baker as needed.
  • Top off before leaving Death Valley.
  • Confirm pump access and correct fuel type for the RV.
Road & weather checks
  • Check Caltrans QuickMap and road closures before each drive.
  • Check NPS park alerts for both parks.
  • Ask Cruise America about chain rules and winter-weather restrictions.
  • Typical late-November days can be mild, but nights are cold; conditions vary.
Roads to avoid with this RV
  • Lower Wildrose Road — 25-ft limit / washout concerns.
  • Artists Drive — 25-ft vehicle restriction.
  • Titus Canyon Road and Geology Tour Road — not suitable for this RV.
  • Any unpaved road or road with a posted length / height restriction that excludes the vehicle.
