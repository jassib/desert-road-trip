Day by day

WED · NOV 25

Santa Clara → Stovepipe Wells

Approx. 470–500 mi · plan 9–11 hr with breaks

Take I‑5 S → CA‑58 E → Barstow → I‑15 N → Baker → CA‑127 N → Shoshone → CA‑190 W to Stovepipe Wells via CA-190 W. Start early and keep the day focused on reaching camp.

Stops: Santa Nella / Buttonwillow for breaks, Barstow for fuel and food, then Baker or Shoshone as needed. Top off before entering the park.

Overnight · Stovepipe Wells
THU · NOV 26

Explore Death Valley

A full park day. Focus on Zabriskie Point for sunrise/sunset badlands, Badwater Basin for the salt flats, and Mesquite Flat Sand Dunes for a walk among the dunes.

Use paved park roads and confirm RV parking at each stop. Skip Artists Drive (25-ft vehicle restriction), Titus Canyon Road and Geology Tour Road.

Overnight · Death Valley
FRI · NOV 27

Stovepipe Wells → 29 Palms RV Resort

Approx. 260 mi · 5.5–6.5 hr with stops

Leave via CA‑190 E → Death Valley Junction → CA‑127 S → Shoshone → Baker → I‑15 S → Barstow → CA‑247 S → Joshua Tree. Keep to the paved southern corridor.

Stops: Shoshone for a break, Baker for fuel/food, and Barstow for supplies. Avoid routing via CA‑178.

Overnight · 29 Palms RV Resort
SAT · NOV 28

Explore Joshua Tree

Prioritize Keys View for the broad Coachella Valley panorama, Hidden Valley for its boulder landscape, and Skull Rock / Jumbo Rocks for classic Joshua Tree scenery.

Check parking and access for a 30-ft RV at each stop before committing. Near-full-moon conditions can make faint stars harder to see.

Overnight · Joshua Tree
SUN · NOV 29

29 Palms Resort → Cambria → Morro Bay → Santa Clara

Recommended return: Central Coast via Paso Robles, Cambria and Morro Bay. This is a long full-day drive; leave very early and keep coastal stops brief.

29 Palms RV Resort → Barstow → CA-58 W → Paso Robles → CA-46 W → Cambria → CA-1 S → Morro Bay → CA-41 E → Atascadero → US-101 N → Santa Clara. Avoid extending north into Big Sur. Check wind, rain, road closures and RV restrictions before setting off.

Recommended return · Central Coast

Places to explore & places to avoid

Simple stop lists for your actual bases and recommended return. “Avoid” includes legal closures, vehicle-length limits and roads unsuitable for this RV; always check posted signs and official updates.

Death Valley · from Stovepipe Wells

Explore
  • Mesquite Flat Sand Dunes — closest major sight to your base
  • Zabriskie Point — sunrise/sunset badlands
  • Badwater Basin — salt flats
  • Harmony Borax Works — short historic stop
  • Golden Canyon — optional hike; verify RV parking
  • Dante’s View — optional longer detour; check wind and RV suitability
Not permitted / avoid with this RV
  • Artists Drive — vehicles over 25 ft prohibited
  • Lower Wildrose Road and Wildrose Campground — 25-ft restrictions
  • Titus Canyon Road, Cottonwood/Marble Roads and Hunter Mountain Road — currently closed
  • Bonnie Clare Road / Scotty’s Castle — closed, including pedestrian access
  • Darwin Falls access road — closed; do not drive the RV onto it
  • Unpaved backcountry roads — not suitable for your rental RV

Joshua Tree · from 29 Palms RV Resort

Explore
  • Oasis of Mara — near Twentynine Palms; stay within open trail sections
  • Keys View — panoramic overlook; confirm RV parking
  • Hidden Valley — boulders and short loop; parking can fill
  • Skull Rock and Jumbo Rocks — roadside rock formations
  • Cap Rock — short nature trail
  • Barker Dam — optional hike; verify parking
  • Cholla Cactus Garden — optional longer paved-road drive
Not permitted / avoid with this RV
  • Geology Tour Road — closed after flooding and not suitable for motorhomes
  • Oasis of Mara trail beyond the open section — currently closed
  • Unpaved 4WD roads, sandy tracks and off-road areas — not for this RV
  • Any trailhead or viewpoint with posted RV restrictions or insufficient parking

Central Coast · return day

Explore
  • Paso Robles downtown — optional coffee/meal stop
  • Moonstone Beach, Cambria — coastal boardwalk
  • Morro Rock — view from shore; climbing prohibited
  • Morro Bay Embarcadero — harbor and food
  • Morro Bay State Park — bay views; confirm day-use RV parking
Not permitted / avoid with this RV
  • Climbing Morro Rock — prohibited
  • Big Sur / CA-1 north of Cambria — not part of this itinerary; avoid detour
  • Narrow coastal shoulders or lots that prohibit oversized RVs
  • Beach access areas closed for surf, flooding or posted restrictions

Before you roll

  • Refuel in Barstow / Baker as needed.
  • Top off before leaving Death Valley.
  • Confirm pump access and correct fuel type for the RV.

  • Check NPS park alerts for both parks.
  • Ask Cruise America about chain rules and winter-weather restrictions.
  • Typical late-November days can be mild, but nights are cold; conditions vary.
Roads to avoid with this RV
